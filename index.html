🎮 How to play and earn

This is a simple guide. Read it once and you will understand everything. 🙌

1️⃣ Create your account

Open the app, fill your first name, last name, username and password. That is your account. Your Telegram ID is connected automatically, so your coins and your account are safe. 🔐

2️⃣ Add coins (deposit)

  • Go to Deposit. Pick a method like Bank, USDT or Telegram Stars.
  • Pay to the details shown on the screen. Press the Copy button so you send to the right account.
  • Type the payment ID or transaction number, attach the payment screenshot, and press Submit.
  • The admin checks it and your coins appear. Usually fast. ⏱️
  • With Telegram Stars there is no address. You just tap and pay from your own Telegram wallet, and coins are added automatically. ⭐

3️⃣ Play the games

Open Play. Pick any game, choose your option and your bet, then press Play. You can see the result straight away, and the app tells you why you won or lost. 🎯

1

Coin Flip - pick HEAD or TAIL. Simple double or nothing.

2

Dice Duel - pick a number 1 to 6 and roll.

3

Crash Rocket - choose a target like 1.5x. The rocket climbs, and you win if it reaches your target before it crashes.

4

Mines - pick a tile. Safe tile means you win, bomb means you lose.

5

Plinko, Wheel, Roulette, Aviator, Keno, Sic Bo - same idea. Pick your option, set your bet, press Play.

Every game shows the animation and the sound while it runs, and the result line explains what happened. 🔊

4️⃣ Take your money out (withdraw)

  • Go to Withdraw, choose a method, type the amount and your account details.
  • Your coins are held safely while the admin processes it.
  • If it is rejected, your coins come back to you automatically. 💯

5️⃣ Earn without playing (referral)

This is the big one. You bring friends and you earn from what they deposit. 💸

1

Open Refer & VIP and copy your invite link.

2

Send it to friends on WhatsApp, Telegram or anywhere.

3

When a friend joins with your link and deposits, you get a % of that deposit as coins.

4

Your friends bring their own friends. You also earn from them, in a chain. This is called your network. 🌳

6️⃣ VIP levels and rewards

  • 🥉 Bronze - refer 10 people, each depositing at least $1. You get 1000 coins and 10% commission.
  • 🥈 Silver - 50 people at $1 or more. You get 10,000 coins and 15% commission.
  • 🥇 Gold - 120 people at $10 or more. You get 70,000 coins and 50% commission.
  • 💎 Diamond - 600 people at $10 or more. You get 300,000 coins and 50% on your whole network.

7️⃣ How the commission chain works

Your commission is the highest on the person you invited directly. It becomes a little smaller for each level deeper, because the reward is shared through the whole chain fairly. Your exact percentages for every level are shown on the Refer & VIP page, so you always know what you will earn. 📊

8️⃣ Need help?

Open Account and use the Support chat. Write your question and the admin replies to you here and in your Telegram chat. 💬

⚠️ Good to know

  • Play only with what you can afford. Games involve luck.
  • A referral only counts after that person actually deposits.
  • Your invite code can be used once and cannot be changed after joining.
